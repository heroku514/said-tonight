Said Tonight keeps a dinner question on the screen in huge type, then saves what someone answered. Kids can take a turn. Grandparents can read the book later. Families can switch between kids, family, and senior questions.

The book stays on this phone. Turn the page, remove one answer, or leave the app and come back.

Said Tonight does not use an account, ads, or a network connection. It is a table book for a conversation you already have.
